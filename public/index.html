Please read the instructions below carefully before you begin

System & Browser

  • Use the latest version of Google Chrome for the best experience. Latest Microsoft Edge or Mozilla Firefox also work; Safari and older browser versions are not recommended.
  • Use a laptop — a desktop or mobile/tablet is not supported. The interview needs reliable camera/microphone access and fullscreen mode, which work most consistently on a laptop.
  • Make sure you have a stable, uninterrupted internet connection for the full duration of the interview. A speed of 5 Mbps upload and 10 Mbps download or better is recommended (minimum 2 Mbps upload / 4 Mbps download). Use a strong Wi-Fi signal or a wired connection, and avoid downloads, streaming or video calls on the same network during the interview.

Camera, Audio & Environment

  • Allow camera and microphone access when prompted — both are required to proceed.
  • Sit directly in front of the camera with your face clearly and fully visible at all times.
  • Make sure the lighting on your face is good — avoid strong backlight (e.g. a window or lamp behind you).
  • Use a plain, single-colour background — a plain white or blue wall is ideal. Avoid busy or cluttered backgrounds.
  • No other person should enter or be visible in the camera frame at any point during the interview.
  • Make sure the room is quiet with no background noise. Wired or noise-cancelling earphones are recommended.

Conduct During the Interview

  • Be seated in one place for the entire interview — please don't get up or walk around mid-interview.
  • Keep your eyes on the laptop screen — avoid frequently looking away or reading from another device/paper.
  • Do not use any external help — books, notes, another device, or another person — while answering.
  • Speak clearly and answer in your own words.

Screen & Session Rules

  • The interview must be taken in fullscreen mode. Exiting fullscreen may be flagged.
  • When you press Begin Interview, your browser will ask to share your screen — choose “This tab” (or the current tab) and click Share. Your screen is recorded for review. A small “Sharing this tab / Stop sharing” indicator from your browser may stay visible — this is normal; please do not click “Stop sharing”.
  • Do not switch tabs, minimize the window, or open other applications during the interview — this may be flagged and can lead to rejection.
  • Complete the interview in a single sitting. Do not close or refresh the browser once started.

Proctoring & Integrity

  • This interview is AI-proctored — your video, audio, and on-screen activity are monitored throughout.
  • Any unethical practice, including impersonation, unauthorized assistance, or attempts to bypass proctoring, may lead to immediate disqualification.
  • By proceeding, you confirm that the responses given are entirely your own.
